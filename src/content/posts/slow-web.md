Some sites load in a blink and ask nothing of you. They do not track, pop up or autoplay. They just show you the words you came for.

Longer posts are cut short on the timeline, with a link through to the full post. This one keeps going on its own page, where the text gets a wider line height and more room to breathe.

## Small pages

A page that weighs a few kilobytes is a kindness to readers on slow connections and old phones.
